Origins

The Story

SKINMETAL was born from the collision between human creativity, Heavy Metal and artificial intelligence. What started as an experiment became a musical and visual universe.

A universe where technology doesn't erase emotion. It intensifies it.

This is not the end of human creativity. This is its next chapter.

The Manifesto

Tecno Metal

Where Technology Meets the Soul of Metal

TECNO METAL combines the raw power, emotion and attitude of Heavy Metal with artificial intelligence, advanced digital creation and cinematic storytelling.

AI is the tool. The human is the soul.

The riffs. The emotions. The stories. The decisions. Technology expands the possibilities. Human creativity gives them meaning.

This is Tecno Metal.
