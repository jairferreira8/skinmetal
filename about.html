About Skinmetal

The New Force of Brazilian Heavy Metal

Conceived in 2025 in the industrial ABC region of São Paulo, SKINMETAL brings the raw force of heavy metal with a sound that's unmistakable, aggressive and real.

Created by engineer and technology enthusiast José Carlos Schiabel, SKINMETAL is Brazil's first Heavy Metal band forged by Humans and AI, combining diverse influences into a sound built to conquer the global scene.

The project explores what happens when technology becomes a creative instrument without replacing the human spirit behind the art.

AI can generate. AI can assist. AI can accelerate.

But metal still needs a soul.

SKINMETAL brings together powerful music, visual storytelling, mythology, technology and human emotion to create a new musical experience.
