About Skinmetal

The New Force of Brazilian Heavy Metal

SKINMETAL is a Brazilian Heavy Metal project built at the intersection of human creativity, music, artificial intelligence and cinematic storytelling.

The project explores what happens when technology becomes a creative instrument without replacing the human spirit behind the art.

AI can generate. AI can assist. AI can accelerate.

But metal still needs a soul.

SKINMETAL brings together powerful music, visual storytelling, mythology, technology and human emotion to create a new musical experience.
